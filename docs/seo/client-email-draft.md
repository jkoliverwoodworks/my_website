**What only you can do (in priority order)**

A lot of local search success comes from things Google wants to hear from the business owner and your customers directly. Here's where to start:

1. **Choose your web address (domain)** (about 15 minutes). The site is still on a temporary address, and this is the one thing holding up everything else. We can suggest options and set it all up; we just need your decision and access to the account where it's registered.
2. **Pick one business phone number** (about 10 minutes). It can be your cell, a second line or a free Google Voice number. Google and customers expect one, and it needs to be the same everywhere.
3. **Set up your Google Business Profile** (about 45 minutes, plus waiting for Google to verify you). This is the listing that shows on Google Maps, and for a local maker it's often the biggest source of new inquiries. The attached guide walks you through it step by step.
4. **Ask past clients for Google reviews** (about 30 minutes to send the messages). The guide includes a ready-to-send text and email. Please don't offer anything in return for reviews, since that's against Google's rules.
5. **Claim a few other listings** (about 1–2 hours in total, spread out over a few weeks): Apple Maps, Bing, Houzz, Yelp, Facebook, Nextdoor and the Buda Chamber.
6. **Keep it going** (about 10 minutes a week, plus a 30-minute monthly check-in): a few new photos and one short update on your Google profile each week, and replies to any reviews.
